What we collect for each venue

Use this as the intake form for partners and the member host. Fields in the order they appear on the snapshot.

  1. Venue name & logo Required

    Official name, plus a square logo (PNG or SVG, 400×400 minimum) supplied by the venue.

  2. Category Required

    Restaurant, Bar or Club. More partnership categories can be added later.

  3. City & area Required

    City from the regional list, plus neighbourhood or building, e.g. DIFC, Gate Village 7.

  4. Cuisine / venue type Required

    Short descriptor, e.g. Indian fine dining, cocktail bar, private members' club.

  5. Website & booking link Required

    Official website and the direct reservation page (SevenRooms, OpenTable or phone).

  6. Description Required

    Free text, 60–120 words. What it is, why a member would go, and one thing to order or ask for.

  7. YPO member offer Required

    Standard benefit: 20% off the food bill (20% off the total bill for bars and clubs), plus terms: validity, group size, exclusions and how to redeem.

  8. Affiliated member Required

    Name, chapter and YPO Connect profile link of the member who owns, invests in or introduced the venue.

  9. Hours & dress code Optional

    Shown in the fact list when supplied.
